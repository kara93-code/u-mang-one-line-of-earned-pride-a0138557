# One Line of Earned Pride

For seven years she taught herself to read by lamplight after the
children slept, and tonight her grandson fell asleep to her voice
reading his bedtime story, every word landing where it belonged.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) assembles the line from
its three beats, checks the contest gate — UTF-8 text, 1 to 240
characters, a single line — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`
as UTF-8, mode 0644.

Entry for make.fun competition

("One Line of Earned Pride").
